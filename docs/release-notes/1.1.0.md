## What's new

- Added configurable **Spanish for Latin America (`es-419`)** alongside English. Choose **Settings → General → Language**, select **Español (Latinoamérica)**, and click **Apply**. Menus, settings, app messages, dates, country names, and agenda summaries follow your choice; system permission prompts and update dialogs follow it after relaunch. Imported titles and names remain intact.
- Music statuses now support an ordered sequence of up to ten standard Slack emojis, including Slack aliases, with drag-to-reorder controls and a 30-second rotation.
- Improved Apple Music status expiration using queued tracks from the same artist, including streaming albums and queue edits.
- Improved meeting contact names and organizer photos, with cache invalidation when Contacts changes and when the app becomes active.
- Added CONCACAF Nations League and UEFA Nations League presets, with more accurate knockout and second-leg fixture durations.

## Novedades

- Soporte configurable de **español latinoamericano**. En **Configuración → General → Idioma**, selecciona **Español (Latinoamérica)** y pulsa **Aplicar**.
- Secuencias de hasta diez emojis para estados de música en Slack, con orden configurable.
- Mejoras en la duración de estados de Apple Music, los contactos de reuniones y las competiciones de selecciones de fútbol.
